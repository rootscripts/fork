What are Riff mobile forks? expand_more

They are independent community projects porting Riff to Android and iOS. Muriel is currently the first available build, with multiple other community forks actively in progress.

Why are multiple forks being made? expand_more

Different community developers experiment with distinct native frameworks, custom UI designs, platform backends, and feature sets for phone and tablet setups.

Are any of these forks official? expand_more

No. None of them are developed, certified, or maintained by Riff's original creator. Stability and updates rely solely on each fork's author.

How do I get other forks once they release? expand_more

As new functional forks for Android or iOS go public, their links, icons, and release repositories will be added directly to the forks list above.

Where do I report bugs or seek help? expand_more

Submit issues and pull requests directly to the GitHub repository of the fork you are using. Do not post mobile issues on the desktop Riff repository.
