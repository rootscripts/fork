How to install

Android

  1. Tap Download for Android.
  2. Open the .apk file. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, then open Riff.

iPhone

  1. Tap Download for iPhone.
  2. iOS can’t open .ipa files by itself. Open it with AltStore, Sideloadly or TrollStore.
  3. If asked, trust the developer in Settings, General, VPN & Device Management.
